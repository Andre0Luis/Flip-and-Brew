# Flip & Brew · Privacy

## Your data stays on the device

Flip & Brew works without an account. Without one, cups, coins, items, mood, daily energy, the profile you fill in (name, phone, age and coffee taste) and preferences stay only on your phone, with no server and no analytics tool.

## Usage access (optional)

If you allow it, the app reads from Android how many times the phone was unlocked and how long the screen was on. These numbers stay on the device and do not show which apps you use. You can revoke the permission at any time in Android settings.

## Motion sensor

The accelerometer is used only to know whether the phone is face down. Readings are not stored or sent anywhere.

## Notifications (optional)

The cup-ready notice is a local notification created on the device itself, and it is off by default.

## Purchases

When buying coins is active, payment is processed by the app store and RevenueCat. The app never sees card details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous installation identifier to record the purchase.

## Delete everything

In Settings, “Delete all data” removes everything the app stored. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.

## Account and backup (optional)

You can create an account with email and password or sign in with Google. If you do, we store your email and a copy of your progress (cups, coins, items, mood, daily energy, the profile you fill in (name, phone, age and coffee taste) and preferences) in Firebase, a Google service, so you can recover it on another device. Only you can access that copy. Google sign-in shares only your profile name and email.

## Deleting the account

In Account › Delete account, we delete your account and the cloud backup. Data on the device stays until you erase it in Settings.
