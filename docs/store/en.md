# Google Play · English

**Title (max 30):** Flip & Brew

**Short description (max 80):**
Put the phone down and let the coffee brew. Digital wellbeing with Stoicism.

**Full description (max 4000):**

Turn your phone face down and a cup of coffee fills in real time. The longer you stay offline, the better the coffee, and every minute becomes a coin to build your shelf.

HOW IT WORKS
• Turn your phone face down (or tap “Start brewing”) and the cup starts to fill.
• Picking up the phone ends the cup. Stopped early? You keep what filled, never zero.
• Earn one coin per offline minute, with a 20% bonus when the cup fills.
• Spend coins on brewers and cups: V60, French press, moka pot, Chemex, mugs and the Stoic Collection.

WELLBEING THAT SHOWS THE PATTERN
• A balance score from 0 to 100, based on your daily goal, finished cups and streak.
• The hours you manage to put the phone down and what tends to interrupt you.
• How you feel after each cup.
• Optional: Android unlocks and screen time, read and kept only on your device.

LEARN
• One short practice a day, without a screen.
• Articles on antifragility (Taleb), Stoicism (Seneca, Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius), digital habits and sleep.
• A quote a day, with context.

STUMBLING TEACHES TOO
A bad day does not erase your progress. The shelf stays, the streak only pauses and the app shows what the day taught.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
No account, no server, no ads. Everything is stored on your phone.

Available in Portuguese, English and Spanish.
