## Google Play

**Título (máx. 30):** Flip & Brew

**Descrição curta (máx. 80, 76):**
Put the phone down and let the coffee brew. Digital wellbeing with Stoicism.

**Descrição completa (máx. 4000, 2028):**

Turn your phone face down and a cup of coffee fills in real time. The longer you stay offline, the better the coffee, and every minute becomes a coin to build your collection.

HOW IT WORKS
• Turn your phone face down (or tap “Start”) and the cup begins to fill.
• Picking up the phone ends the cup. Stopped early? You keep what filled, never zero.
• Earn one coin per offline minute, plus a 20% bonus when the cup fills.
• Trade coins for brewers (Melitta, V60, French press, moka, Chemex, espresso and more), cups, mugs, glasses and coffees, from extra-strong to specialty. Every combination earns a coin bonus.
• Special collections: Stoic, Mountain, Corner Bar, Night, Gold, Universe and your team's colors.

WELLBEING THAT SHOWS THE PATTERN
• A 0 to 100 balance score, from your daily goal, finished cups and streak.
• A daily energy check-in on a cup scale, with history and a month calendar.
• The hours you manage to put the phone down, which days work best and what usually interrupts you.
• Daily missions with coin rewards.
• Optional: Android unlocks and screen time, read and kept only on your device.

LEARN
• One short, screen-free practice a day.
• One hundred articles on coffee, brewing methods, taste, health, ritual, Stoicism, antifragility, digital habits and sleep.
• A quote a day, with context.

WIDGETS
Quote of the day, cup in progress, streak and coins, today's goal and missions, on the home screen and the lock screen.

STUMBLING TEACHES TOO
A bad day does not erase your progress. The collection stays, the streak only pauses and the app shows what the day taught.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
The app works without an account and without ads, and everything is stored on your phone. If you want, create an account (email, Google or Apple) to back up your progress in the cloud and restore it on another device. You can delete the account and the backup at any time, inside the app.

Coin purchases are optional: you can use the whole app without paying.

Available in Portuguese, English and Spanish.
